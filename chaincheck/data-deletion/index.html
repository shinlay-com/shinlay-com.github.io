ChainCheck - Delete your data

Applies to: ChainCheck for Android, package com.shinlay.chaincheck.

There is no account to delete

ChainCheck has no accounts and no ChainCheck server. All of your data is stored only on your device, so there is no server-side account or profile to delete.

How to delete your data

  1. Open ChainCheck, then go to Settings and tap Erase all ChainCheck data. This removes your saved check history, saved recipients and labels, settings and appearance, and any imported threat dataset.
  2. Or uninstall the app, which removes all ChainCheck data from your device.

What is deleted

  • Saved scan and check history
  • Saved recipients and labels
  • Settings, appearance, and provider preferences
  • Any imported threat dataset

What is kept

Nothing is kept by us. ChainCheck collects and stores no data on a server, so there is no copy for us to retain. The identifiers you choose to check are sent to the third-party blockchain data provider only to read public chain data (see the privacy policy). Any metric handling by Google ML Kit is governed by Google's policies.

Retention period

Deletion is immediate and permanent. ChainCheck retains no data.
