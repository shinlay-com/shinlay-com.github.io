ChainCheck Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 September 2026
Publisher: Shinlay · Contact: support@shinlay.com
Applies to: ChainCheck for Android, package app.chaincheck.chaincheck.

Summary

ChainCheck is a local-first blockchain check tool. It has no accounts, no ChainCheck backend, no wallet connection, and no ads. It never asks for seed phrases, private keys, or signing authority, and it never moves your funds.

Most of what ChainCheck does happens on your device. The one time data leaves your device is when you ask ChainCheck to read public blockchain data: the address, transaction, or contract you chose to check is sent to a third-party blockchain data provider so the result can be retrieved.

What stays on your device

ChainCheck stores the following only on your device, protected by the operating system's app storage:

  • scan and check history you choose to save;
  • saved recipients (addresses you explicitly confirmed);
  • settings, appearance, and network/provider preferences;
  • any threat dataset you import.

ChainCheck does not upload this data to us or anyone else. It is removed when you uninstall the app, or when you use the in-app Erase all control.

What leaves your device when you run a check

When you choose to check an address, payment request, token, contract, or transaction, ChainCheck sends that identifier (for example an address or a transaction hash) to the blockchain data provider selected for that network, over HTTPS, so it can read public chain data. That provider sees your IP address and the identifier you queried.

Built-in default providers:

  • Ethereum / Base: dRPC (https://eth.drpc.org, https://base.drpc.org)
  • Bitcoin: mempool.space (https://mempool.space/api)
  • Testnets: PublicNode / dRPC testnet endpoints

You can change the provider in Settings. These are independent third parties with their own privacy policies and terms; ChainCheck does not control them. ChainCheck does not send your saved history, recipients, labels, or settings to any provider — only the specific identifier you asked to check.

Camera and QR codes

Scanning uses the device camera. Camera frames and decoded QR contents are processed on-device using Google ML Kit's barcode scanning; images are not uploaded. ML Kit may send performance and usage metrics (such as device and app information, per-installation identifiers, performance metrics, and error codes) to Google, as described in the ML Kit terms. Auto-zoom is not enabled in ChainCheck.

Clipboard

ChainCheck reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste. It does not read the clipboard in the background, and it does not transmit clipboard contents until you start a check.

Analytics

Optional analytics is off by default. If you turn it on, ChainCheck records a small, fixed set of local events (allow-listed, no addresses, no labels, no history). In this version there is no analytics backend, so nothing is transmitted. You can turn it off at any time.

Diagnostics you export

If you export a diagnostic bundle or payment evidence, ChainCheck redacts sensitive local annotations and secrets, and the export happens only when you initiate it. Nothing is sent automatically.

What ChainCheck never does

  • It never asks for or stores seed phrases or private keys.
  • It never connects to your wallet or signs transactions.
  • It never sells your data or shows ads.
  • It does not upload your saved history, recipients, labels, or settings.

Third-party services

  • Blockchain data providers (see above) — used to read public chain data.
  • Google ML Kit — on-device barcode scanning; metrics as described in the ML Kit terms.

Data retention and deletion

Because ChainCheck stores your data locally, you can delete it at any time with the in-app Erase all control, or by uninstalling the app. We hold no server copy of your data.

Children

ChainCheck is not directed to children and is intended for users aged 18 and over.

Security

Network requests use HTTPS. Local data is stored in the app's private storage. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so keep your device protected.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and the new policy published at the same URL.
